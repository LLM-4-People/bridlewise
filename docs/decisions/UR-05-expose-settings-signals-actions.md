# Expose settings, signals and actions

Every feature registers its settings (scoped), signals (observable state and
events) and actions (invocable operations) through the one exposure registry,
backend and frontend, so users, the UI, agents and automation can observe and
drive it. Designs and writer reports list what they expose.
