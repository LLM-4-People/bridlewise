# What the browser lab may use: "everything needed"

Agents may copy the lab's Windows bundle to `C:` through WSL and launch it
for automated checks (interactive checks are batched for the user); may
spend up to $20 of Coral in this lab (a hard cap), the key reaching the
process from the environment only and never printed or recorded
([D-09](D-09-credentials-never-in-repository-records.md)); may start and
stop the lab's own server; and may trim superseded bundles, keeping the
current bundle and every receipt.
